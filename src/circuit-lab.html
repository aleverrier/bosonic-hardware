Circuit schematic

Symbols and connections
C, Ca, Cb
Capacitance of a resonator; parallel plates.
L, La, Lb, Ls
Inductance; coil. Ls is the ATS inductive shunt.
JJ, JJL, JJR
Josephson junction; cross.
Cc, Cd, Cext
Capacitive coupling between modes, to a drive, or to an external port.
50 Ω; κa, κb
External microwave environment; mode energy decay rates. The termination represents the environment beyond the coupling port.
ΦL, ΦR; dashed arrow
Flux through the two ATS loops; magnetic flux pump, rather than an electrical wire.
εb
Coherent microwave drive applied to the buffer.
